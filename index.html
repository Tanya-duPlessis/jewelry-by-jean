ABOUT

Inhouse Goldsmith

Hi all, I am Jean. I have been in the jewelry industry for 20 years. I started in sales at the local jewelry store and was fascinated by what the goldsmith in the store was doing. I fell in love with the process of creating jewelry and decided to go and study goldsmithing. After qualifying I made the decision to work for myself. To say I was petrified is an understatement! Hard work and sticking with it helped fuel the passion I have for the industry, and 7 years later I can truly say I am happy. I love what I do, and I love my customers. Jewelry is a very personal purchase and I strive to make every piece worthy. There is a piece of my soul in every piece I create.
